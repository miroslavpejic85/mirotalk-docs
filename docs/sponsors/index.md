## Two ways to sponsor

| Path | Best for | Source of terms |
| --- | --- | --- |
| GitHub Sponsors | Individuals and organizations choosing a published recurring or one-time tier | [Current GitHub sponsor tiers](https://github.com/sponsors/miroslavpejic85) |
| Company sponsorship | Organizations proposing a tailored ecosystem placement or technical collaboration | Direct written proposal and agreement |

Tier names, prices, benefits, availability, and payment terms can change. GitHub Sponsors is the source of truth for its published tiers; a company sponsorship is not active until its scope and terms are confirmed in writing.

## Relevant technology categories

Potential sponsor alignment includes:

- cloud, VPS, networking, and hosting;
- WebRTC infrastructure and media services;
- recording, transcription, translation, and AI;
- security, identity, privacy, and abuse prevention;
- monitoring, observability, testing, and developer tools;
- databases, object storage, content delivery, and backups;
- communications APIs and complementary integration platforms.

Category relevance does not guarantee acceptance. MiroTalk may decline sponsorships that do not fit the project or its users.

## Company sponsorship proposals

A useful proposal identifies:

1. the company, product, and relevant landing page;
2. how the technology relates to MiroTalk users or contributors;
3. the requested placement, campaign period, or technical activity;
4. the assets and destination links involved;
5. the budget, timeline, and contact responsible for approval;
6. any tracking, disclosure, review, or reporting requirements.

Audience size, placement, exclusivity, deliverables, performance reporting, and renewal are not assumed. Confirm them in the sponsorship agreement before announcing a campaign.

## Sponsorship and technical claims

Sponsor placements should be identifiable as sponsorship where appropriate. A sponsorship does not imply that MiroTalk guarantees, certifies, or supports a sponsor's product unless a separate published statement explicitly says so. Technical integrations should be validated against the relevant MiroTalk product and documented limitations.

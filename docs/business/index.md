## Common solution patterns

### Video SaaS and marketplaces

Build accounts, room creation, scheduling, invitations, and eligible paid access around MiroTalk WEB, then connect the media application that matches the meeting model. Use the published APIs and webhooks where supported instead of assuming every application exposes the same integration surface.

### Education and professional consultations

Use SFU for server-routed group sessions, P2P for smaller mesh meetings, or C2C for focused one-to-one calls. Organizations in regulated sectors remain responsible for evaluating privacy, security, data handling, retention, consent, and compliance requirements for their deployment.

### Customer support and sales

Use CME when a visitor should see agent availability and start a call, or C2C when the workflow needs a direct one-to-one room. Embed the experience in an existing site or create links from the host application.

### Agencies and integrators

Deliver a branded client experience using documented integration and rebranding paths. Confirm licensing for each end product and define who owns infrastructure, updates, monitoring, third-party services, and ongoing support before delivery.

### Webinars and live events

Use SFU when participants need an interactive server-routed room, or BRO for a broadcaster-and-viewer model. Capacity depends on topology, media settings, recording, streaming, network conditions, and infrastructure; validate representative load before launch.

## Hosted or self-hosted?

| Choose | When it fits | Your responsibility |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [MiroTalk Cloud](../cloud/index.md) | You want to use MiroTalk without operating its infrastructure | Configure and use the available managed service |
| [Self-hosted MiroTalk](../self-host/index.md) | You need infrastructure control, source-level adaptation, or a private operating model | Deployment, security, capacity, backups, monitoring, updates, and third-party services |

Self-hosting provides control, not automatic compliance or unlimited scale. Your organization decides whether the selected architecture and operating controls meet its requirements.

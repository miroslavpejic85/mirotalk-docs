Frequently asked

MiroTalk vs Zoom questions

Is MiroTalk a self-hosted Zoom alternative?

Yes. MiroTalk provides self-hosted products for group meetings, P2P rooms, one-to-one calls, click-to-call, broadcasting, scheduling, and administration. It is not a clone of every Zoom Workplace feature.

Can MiroTalk use my own domain and branding?

Yes, when deployed on your infrastructure. Commercial licenses provide a documented rebranding path for eligible end products. Review the controlling license terms before distribution.

Does MiroTalk charge per user?

Self-hosted MiroTalk does not use Zoom's per-user subscription model. You remain responsible for infrastructure and operations, and commercial use may require an applicable product license.

Is Zoom easier to operate?

Usually, because Zoom operates the managed platform. MiroTalk gives you more control in exchange for owning deployment, security, monitoring, upgrades, and capacity planning.

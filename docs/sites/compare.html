An honest recommendation

When MiroTalk is the right choice, and when it is not.

A credible comparison should make the trade-off visible. Self-hosting creates freedom, but it also creates responsibility.

Choose MiroTalk when...

You need source access, infrastructure control, custom branding, a specialized communication workflow, or a route from open development to a private commercial product.

Choose MiroTalk Cloud when...

You want the MiroTalk experience without operating servers. The managed workspace provides accounts, scheduling, rooms, and access to supported communication products.

Choose a hosted suite when...

Your priority is immediate adoption inside Google Workspace or Microsoft 365, broad enterprise procurement, or a familiar turnkey meeting client.

Choose another open platform when...

Your team already operates Jitsi successfully, needs its specific ecosystem, or prefers its architecture and community over MiroTalk's focused product suite.

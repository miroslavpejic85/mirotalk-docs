## Choose a collaboration path

| Partner type | Potential collaboration |
| --- | --- |
| Software agencies | Design, integrate, customize, deploy, and support MiroTalk-based client projects |
| SaaS builders | Add MiroTalk communication workflows to a vertical or horizontal software product |
| System integrators | Connect identity, business applications, infrastructure, and operational workflows |
| Hosting companies | Develop repeatable deployment and managed-infrastructure offerings |
| Consultants and WebRTC developers | Support architecture, implementation, testing, optimization, and operations |
| Technology vendors | Validate integrations that complement media, recording, transcription, security, observability, or storage workflows |

These are collaboration directions, not a published certification or reseller program. Scope, responsibilities, commercial terms, and public representation are agreed directly for each engagement.

## What partners can build

- embedded meetings, direct calls, click-to-call, broadcasting, or scheduling inside an existing product;
- branded deployments for eligible client use cases;
- repeatable infrastructure and operations packages;
- connectors to authentication, calendars, CRM, support, billing, automation, or analytics systems;
- validated integrations with complementary infrastructure and developer services.

Start with the [business solutions](../business/index.md), [product chooser](../projects/index.md), and [developer documentation](../build/index.md) to identify the relevant MiroTalk applications.

## What MiroTalk provides

Depending on the selected product and agreed collaboration, partners can work from:

- open-source repositories and commercial source distributions;
- product-specific deployment and configuration guides;
- documented embedding, direct-join, API, and webhook surfaces;
- published licensing options and white-label documentation;
- direct discussion for non-standard commercial or technical requirements.

No service level, lead sharing, exclusivity, certification, discount, or support entitlement is implied unless it is included in a separate written agreement.

## Prepare a partnership proposal

Include:

1. your organization, capabilities, and relevant delivery experience;
2. the customers, market, or technical workflow you plan to serve;
3. the MiroTalk products and integration model you are evaluating;
4. the proposed responsibilities for sales, implementation, infrastructure, support, and maintenance;
5. the collaboration outcome and next step you are requesting.

Do not include customer secrets, credentials, private room links, or production configuration in an introductory message.
